# Change: Clarify learning-content hierarchy

## Why

Practice questions and knowledge prose compete visually with configuration and support
surfaces. The learning material should be the stable reading anchor, while mode
selection, answer entry, solutions, and related-problem navigation remain quieter.

## What Changes

- Give active questions and knowledge prose a larger reading rhythm and one structural
  accent rule.
- Flatten their shadows so typography, rather than card chrome, carries emphasis.
- Recess mode selection and revealed solutions.
- Increase separation before related problems and keep each topic compact.

No practice phase, action, or learning write changes.
