# Design — problem difficulty and provenance

## Data contract

`problems` is rebuilt once because the existing INTEGER affinity is no longer
the authoritative shape. `difficulty` becomes REAL and is accompanied by four
nullable INTEGER dimensions and `difficulty_model`. A table CHECK enforces
all-null or all-present. Migration clears old scalar values rather than
inventing dimensions.

The four dimensions apply to formal problems and micro quizzes only. Flash
cards have no objective problem difficulty. `knowledge_points.difficulty`
remains a legacy content-complexity value and is not another representation of
the problem vector.

`origin_kind` is required with values `source_problem`, `adapted_problem`, or
`generated_grounded`; `source_kind` retains the underlying material class.
Generated origin wins the convenience group, making the three common filters
mutually exclusive.

The complete convenience-group derivation is:

- `ai_generated` when `origin_kind=generated_grounded`;
- `exam` for every other row whose source is quiz, midterm, final, or makeup;
- `textbook` for every other row whose source is textbook;
- `other` for the remainder.

The two stored axes remain independently filterable and may be intersected.
An AI-generated problem grounded in a final exam is therefore AI-generated by
group while still retaining `source_kind=final`.

## Dimension rubric

Every dimension is an integer from 1 to 5. A problem type never determines a
score by itself: a routine proof may be low, and a transfer-heavy multiple-
choice item may be high. Knowledge breadth follows the necessary knowledge
structure rather than the raw `kp_ids` count; reasoning depth follows the
shortest reliable solution rather than answer length.

| Score | Knowledge breadth | Reasoning depth | Transfer distance | Construction openness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One local fact or concept | Recall, recognition, or one direct substitution | Isomorphic to a definition or worked example | Selection, judgement, or one unique short answer |
| 2 | One concept plus a direct prerequisite, or two same-section points | One standard transformation or calculation | Only values, wording, or surface context change | Bounded calculation or short explanation |
| 3 | Two or three same-chapter concepts cooperate | Dependent steps including one method choice | Representation or combination is unfamiliar but the method family is clear | Goal is fixed but several valid paths exist |
| 4 | Several concepts across sections | Multi-stage reasoning that organizes intermediate results and methods | No method cue; the solver must identify and adapt one | Proof, modelling, or design contains a substantive choice |
| 5 | Cross-chapter or cross-framework synthesis | Subgoals and methods must be planned and may require backtracking | Method must be generalized or reconstructed for new conditions or a new domain | Constraints are incomplete and alternatives must be weighed and justified |

## Pure score

`workbench.domain.difficulty` owns `cognitive-v1-equal-mean`. It validates four
integer values from 1 to 5, averages them with `Decimal`, and quantizes to one
decimal with `ROUND_HALF_UP`. No caller-provided total, model, or prose basis
is accepted.

## Lazy rating transaction

One JSON manifest contains 1-N items with `problem_id` and exactly the four
dimensions. `check` validates and previews with zero writes. `apply` validates
every item, then overwrites all rows in one transaction. Existing ratings do
not require a separate delete or force flag. Edits to problem text, solution,
knowledge-point membership, or problem type clear the six rating fields.

Rating is deliberately lazy. Creation, ingest, display, practice, and
scheduling never depend on it. No read, content write, background task, or new
problem prompt queues or suggests a rating. Pi may run check then apply only in
response to explicit learner intent to rate a bounded problem set.

## Pull and planning

No difficulty/provenance argument means byte-for-byte-compatible selection.
Explicit ranges exclude unrated rows. `balanced` rounds totals half-up to five
bands and cycles `3,2,4,1,5`, preserving the existing order inside a band;
unrated rows follow as fallback. Plan JSON may carry distributions and a
suggested mix, but pages do not render them.

Personal evidence continues to choose knowledge scope and due priority. It
does not create a learner ability level, change the objective vector, or
silently raise/lower requested objective difficulty after one self-rating.

## Compatibility

The repository dmath pool is disposable test data. Frozen pipeline inserts
continue through a default `source_problem` origin; workbench Agent-created
content must declare both provenance axes. No production pool is migrated.

The table rebuild must preserve existing problem type/source constraints,
indexes, rows, and child foreign keys with `PRAGMA foreign_keys=ON`; an empty
`foreign_key_check` and idempotent second ensure are acceptance conditions.

## Acceptance boundary

The design and OpenSpec contract are frozen in this change. Automated full-
repository verification, disposable-pool migration, and live acceptance are
deliberately left to the next Agent and remain unchecked in `tasks.md`. That
Agent must not run the migration against an external or real learner pool.
