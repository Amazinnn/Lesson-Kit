# Why

The persistent streak badge (number and flames) stays beside ordinary questions even when no celebration is occurring. The learner wants the streak to appear only as a transient combo banner at its milestone.

# What Changes

- Remove the persistent streak number and flame badge from Paper and ordinary practice question cards.
- Keep the in-memory consecutive-correct count and the existing five-answer banner triggers. Paper keeps its approved pixel banner; ordinary practice keeps its shared banner.
- Preserve verdict, attempts, completion and navigation behavior. A streak break may still give its existing short card nudge without restoring the badge.

# Scope

Only workbench presentation and its user-facing contracts change. No request or persistence changes.
