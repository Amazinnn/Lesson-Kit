## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: Standing learner requests authorize proactive experience capture
An explicit standing request SHALL qualify as an explicit request for experience writes within its scope until revoked. Under it the Agent SHALL identify mature reusable methods, distinctions and strategies, read existing experience, merge without duplication and report a confirmed successful save. Speculation and every conversational turn SHALL NOT automatically become experience. Ambiguous ownership or conclusions SHALL require clarification. Existing revision checks and evidence constraints SHALL remain.
#### Scenario: Authorized reusable conclusion
- **WHEN** a reusable conclusion is established under a standing request
- **THEN** the Agent updates its appropriate knowledge-point experience without redundant approval and reports actual outcome
